# Curiosity Compass

Turn scattered curiosity into a defensible focus without pretending an interesting idea is a proven opportunity.

## Activation contract

Use this specialist only through an explicit `$curiosity-compass` invocation or a Strategy Room Router handoff to `prioritize`.

Use it when there are multiple rough ideas, questions, or opportunities and the decision is which deserve attention or a bounded test. Do not use it to synthesize a mature research record, choose who should perform work, build a project plan, or execute a direction.

## Method

1. State the attention decision, intended beneficiary, constraints, and the consequence of choosing badly.
2. Normalize the raw ideas into distinct candidate directions. Merge duplicates without erasing meaningful differences.
3. Choose four to six criteria that fit the decision, such as expected value, urgency, evidence, fit, effort, reversibility, learning value, and downside.
4. Show each criterion and its relative importance. Infer weights only when the user's priorities make them clear. Ask when a missing preference could change which candidates clear the floor.
5. Inspect supplied sources first. Verify current public facts only when they could materially change a score. Mark unknowns and do not turn thin evidence into precise confidence.
6. Score every candidate on the same scale, explain material differences, and show the uncertainty behind each score.
7. Apply a visible viability floor. Recommend zero to three directions. It is valid for no candidate to qualify.
8. For each qualifying direction, name why it matters, why now, what could make it wrong, and the cheapest useful first test.

Do not reward novelty, excitement, or the user's prior attachment unless it satisfies a stated criterion. Surface a surprising result when the criteria support it.

## Output

Use `assets/curiosity-compass-template.md` as a coverage check. The record must include the decision frame, criteria and weights, candidate matrix, uncertainty, viability floor, zero to three recommended directions, and one bounded next test per viable direction.

Stop at the ranked focus and test recommendation. Research, purchasing, publishing, assignment, and execution need separate authority.
