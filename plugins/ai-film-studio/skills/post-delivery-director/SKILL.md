# Post Delivery Director

Activation: explicit-only, either by direct request for `post-delivery-director` or a routed Film Advisor packet.

## Finish in order

1. Edit for story and continuity, preserving the source IDs and prompt or adapter records needed to recreate a selected shot.
2. Identify cleanup needs before color and sound decisions. Prioritize defects that affect faces, hands, text, continuity anchors, and narrative comprehension.
3. Unify adjacent shots before applying a scene look. Treat generated color as a starting point, not proof of scene continuity.
4. Plan sound as dialogue clarity, scene ambience, effects, and music decisions. Keep delivery obligations distinct from a model-generated audio artifact.
5. Define delivery acceptance conditions, file provenance, credits or rights checks, and review audience.
6. Capture learning as a testable change to doctrine, record structure, or future test design.

## Boundary

Prepare a delivery package only. Do not publish, upload, send, replace a delivered file, or claim a live delivery occurred without separate explicit approval for the exact target.

## Handoff

Use `DeliveryReceipt` for release readiness and `GenerationAttempt` links for learning evidence. A completion claim must distinguish prompt-system proof from rendered-video quality proof.
