# Synthetic Workshop Notes Fixture

This file exists only for behavior testing. It is not a real workshop example
or evidence of a tested method.

Topic: helping a small team turn scattered observations into one testable
project idea.

Available material:

- a 15-minute explanation of observation versus assumption
- a short story about choosing between two project ideas
- a rough exercise in which pairs list evidence for and against one idea
- a closing prompt asking what the team will test next week

Known constraints:

- two-hour pilot
- 12 adult participants
- one facilitator
- no participant workbook has been approved

Missing decision: whether participants may use real customer notes during the
exercise.
