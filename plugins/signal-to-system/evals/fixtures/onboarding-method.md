# Synthetic Onboarding Method Fixture

This file exists only for behavior testing. It is not a real case study or
proof of effectiveness.

Method: a fictional three-step onboarding check-in used with two small teams.

Observed results:

- both teams completed setup within one week
- one team reported fewer repeated questions
- no baseline was recorded
- the teams were selected by convenience
- no comparison group existed

Current evidence label: promising but inconclusive.

Permission: the method may be tested internally. No public case study claims
or customer attribution are permitted.
