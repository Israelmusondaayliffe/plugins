# Image Prompt Create

Read the shared [prompt contract](../../references/prompt-contract.md). Use [model profiles](../../references/model-profiles.md) when adapting a prompt for a named model.

Turn the brief into a specific visual moment. Establish subject, action or state, environment, framing and the light or graphic treatment that makes the idea read. Explain materials through their behavior: transmission, reflections, wear, weight, folds, surface contact. Choose photographic terms only for a photographic treatment; preserve impossible or absurd premises when intentional.

For exploration, distinguish open, directed and partially directed requests. Open work can move between conceptual territories; directed work varies the named dimension; partially directed work explores nearby possibilities. A coherent shoot can contain several distinct frames without changing its whole aesthetic. Use the creative-direction library when ideas become repetitive, not as a mandatory checklist.

For portraits, match texture specificity to viewing distance and preserve supplied features. For products, describe construction, finish, scale, support and reflected surroundings. Stickers/icons need an intentional background treatment; do not promise transparency unless supported. Keep exact copy explicit when present. Finish with the requested framing/ratio and a small number of relevant constraints.

Read [creation recipes](references/gpt-create.md) and [additional creation recipes](references/nano-create.md) for general scenes, portraits, negative space, camera simulation, stickers and icons. The image-architect and Uni recipes preserve their detailed prompt structures. For inspiration use the shared [creative directions](../../references/patterns/creative-directions.md), [use cases](../../references/patterns/use-case-patterns.md), or [texture vocabulary](../../references/patterns/texture-library.md).

For detailed alternatives, read [architect creation](references/architect-create.md) or [Uni creation](references/uni-create.md). When the brief calls for them, use [fashion construction vocabulary](../../references/patterns/fashion-vocabulary.md), [architect creative directions](../../references/patterns/architect-creative-directions.md), [Uni creative directions](../../references/patterns/uni-creative-directions.md), or the [Uni use-case catalog](../../references/patterns/uni-use-case-catalog.md). These are optional source libraries, not a required aesthetic.
