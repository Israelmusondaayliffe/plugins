# Illustration and photo translator

Study the supplied reference and carry its recognizable character, object or world into the target medium. Support both directions equally. The reference takes priority over a generic realism recipe.

Read the [prompt contract](../../references/prompt-contract.md) for counts, formats, reference handling and delivery. When a model is named, consult only its relevant [model profile](../../references/model-profiles.md).

## Translate the visual idea

Inspect every reference used. Identify what gives the subject its visual identity: defining shapes and proportions, expression or attitude, materials, color relationships, props, composition, mood and the world's rules. Use a supplied illustration-style reference for its assigned treatment; keep content and style roles clear.

Read [two-way methods and working templates](references/two-way-methods.md) for character, object and world handling, natural language, JSON and system-style forms. The [original human conversion templates](references/original-human-conversion-templates.md) preserve the detailed one-way source for cases where it fits; their human fields and restrictive realism choices are not a universal contract.

For illustration to photo, translate edges, marks, flat fills and simplified depth into a coherent photographic treatment. Preserve stylized proportions and fictional premises when they define the subject. A strange character or impossible world can still have believable materials and light.

For photo to illustration, translate observed form and atmosphere into the target's mark-making, shape language, palette, texture and depth. Preserve the content's identity without demanding every pore or photographic shadow survive unchanged.

Use judgment for ambiguous details and backgrounds. Make the smallest useful interpretation, or offer a few faithful treatments when the direction is open. Do not require an intake taxonomy, score fidelity numerically, default every conversion to fashion, or force pose changes. Obey requested count and format.

## Deliver and review

Return complete copyable prompts, with concise interpretation notes only when they help the user. If outputs are supplied, compare recognizability and medium translation to the actual reference; describe meaningful drift without turning examples into a mechanical checklist. Do not declare a preferred recipe or creative acceptance for the user.
