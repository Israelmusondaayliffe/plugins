# Third-party assets

This release keeps only assets with an inspectable redistribution basis.

- The 44 standard caption font files and the Fredoka template font are byte-identical to the matching `@fontsource` 5.3.0 package files. The exact family license texts are in `third-party-licenses/fontsource/`.
- The 66 texture-mask PNG files are byte-identical to `heygen-com/hyperframes` commit `b9233525b2e02ec9329445ab2602ebd557033daf`. Hyperframes is Apache-2.0 licensed, and the license text is in `third-party-licenses/hyperframes-apache-2.0.txt`.
- The six Graphic Overlays font files are byte-identical to `notedit/vtake-skills` commit `ee5b4ab43da79e0b33a71f5577df0a701cbb2d62`. The retained MIT notice is in `skills/graphic-overlays/NOTICE.md`.
- The Hershey stroke-font SVG files carry their redistribution terms and required acknowledgements inside each SVG file.
- Website-to-video sound effects are documented in `skills/website-to-video/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`.
- The Playwright image assets remain beside their upstream `LICENSE.txt` and `NOTICE.txt`.

`CyberpunkReplica.ttf` is not distributed because the available copy is licensed for personal use only. The setup note in `skills/embedded-captions/assets/brand/PUBLIC-RELEASE.md` requires users to supply their own validly licensed copy.
