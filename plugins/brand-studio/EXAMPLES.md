# Three example tasks

These prompts are adapted from real work patterns. They are reusable demonstrations, not claims that this public version produced the original work. Provide the stated inputs before running each example.

## 1. Turn an editorial campaign request into a brand brief

Inputs: Provide the selected brand, approved brand references and the campaign request.

```text
Use brand-studio:brand-brief to turn this fashion-editorial request into a practical brief. It needs vertical campaign images with one consistent model, varied angles and emotions, and a different approved product in each frame. Separate supplied brand facts from your proposed creative choices. Preserve the selected brand and mark missing evidence. Deliver the brief, not image prompts.
```

Expected result: A campaign brief with audience, purpose, brand constraints, visual direction and open questions.

## 2. Develop visual rules for a second brand

Inputs: Provide the second brand's references and the existing campaign constraints.

```text
Use brand-studio:brand-identity-system to propose visual rules for this second brand. Keep the campaign to close-ups, profiles and mid-body framing. Explain the palette, materials, lighting and composition that are supported by the references. Identify what should change from the first campaign and what should remain consistent. Do not invent official brand rules or generate images.
```

Expected result: Usable visual guidelines that distinguish source-backed rules from proposals.

## 3. Review campaign concepts for consistency

Inputs: Provide the approved brief and the written campaign concepts.

```text
Use brand-studio:brand-review on these campaign concepts against the approved brief. Check brand fit, product treatment, framing, palette and consistency across the series. Quote the relevant requirement for each problem and suggest the smallest repair. Flag unsupported assumptions. Review the concepts only; do not generate or publish anything.
```

Expected result: A prioritised review linked to the brief, with concrete repairs and remaining unknowns.
